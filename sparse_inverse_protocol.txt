# Sparse verified inverse experiment

The experiment uses two public SNAP graphs. Symmetrize the edge list, remove self loops, collapse duplicates, and keep the largest connected component. Sort adjacency indices and keep the induced subgraph of the first vertices in breadth-first order from the smallest vertex identifier. Use 512 vertices from ca-GrQc with shift 2^-5 and 768 vertices from Email-Eu-core with shift 2^-1. The two shifts and dimensions are specified before running. The public source bytes are pinned by SHA-256.

- [SNAP ca-GrQc](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/ca-GrQc.html)
- [SNAP Email-Eu-core](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/email-Eu-core.html)

For the simple undirected graph with degrees d_i and shift ell > 0, K is its combinatorial Laplacian plus ell I. The energy identity makes ell the exact smallest eigenvalue and 2 max_i d_i + ell a certified upper bound on the largest eigenvalue. The reported condition bound is the ratio of these spectral bounds. The recorded dense binary64 eigenvalue estimate is diagnostic. The timed verified matrix action uses edge differences with Arb at 256-bit precision, so applying K to a trial vector uses linear work in the graph size. Galerkin storage and reorthogonalization still use dense basis vectors. The independently computed dense Arb inverse is used only to audit intervals and is timed separately.

Run the origin-anchored progress interval at stake 1/3 and frozen norm at stake 1/2 in separate executions. Both use the same dyadic Gaussian bins on each of three paired seeds 11304000 through 11304002 for ca-GrQc and 11305000 through 11305002 for Email-Eu-core. Alternate the order across case and seed index. Each method uses risk 0.05 and a target relative radius of 0.10. At most 144 outer probes are allowed. Each probe starts a binary64 CG solve at rtol 0.01 and attempts tighter warm starts at 1e-4, 1e-7, and 1e-10 as needed. The projection check uses eta² ≤ delta and xi ≤ 3/80. A failed final check retains the outward inexact evidence. A direction with uncertified pivot energy is skipped. Each execution ends at its own first outward 10% certificate, full span, or the cap. CG iteration counts include restarts. The cap exceeds neither graph dimension.

Time each run from before the first probe through its exit, including Gaussian enclosures, CG, projection, evidence and output endpoint construction. Record time per phase, total wall time, certified pivot count, failed quality gates, solver status, and reference containment checks. Common graph construction, independent audit inversion and warm-up are outside the timed run. The reference is used for diagnostics and never sets intervals or chooses whether to stop. The seeded experiment measures an implementation and uses public real inputs. The stochastic theorem assumes ideal independent Gaussian bits. Three paired seeds per graph quantify the observed diagnostic behavior.
